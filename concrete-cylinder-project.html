Concrete Cylinder Compression Lab

EGN-301, UNCW, Spring 2026

Overview

I tested a concrete cylinder to failure on a Universal Testing Machine (UTM) to determine its compressive strength, Young's modulus, and lateral deflection, then compared the experimental results to theoretical values to trace down sources of error.

Methodology

The cylinder was loaded in compression on the UTM while load and displacement data were recorded and exported for analysis. I processed the load-displacement data in Excel to calculate the stress-strain relationship and identify the elastic, yield, and ultimate stress regions.

Results & Discussion

The experimental Young's modulus fell short of theoretical concrete values. I traced the gap to the specimen's short 7-day curing time and its alignment in the test rig.
